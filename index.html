Rona Classroom+

The teacher's toolkit for building exercises, running class games, tracking points and keeping grades — all in one comfortable place.

Everything you teach, in one classroom

Rona Classroom+ extends what you can do in a lesson. Build your own exercises, turn them into games, reward your students live, and keep every grade in order.

📝

Build any exercise

Multiple choice, true/false, typing, underlining words, matching pairs, filling blanks or putting things in order. Seven formats, your content.

⭐

Live class points

Add your students with their own avatars, give or take points instantly, and let the random picker choose who answers next.

🎮

Turn it into a game

Team boards, spin the wheel, match-up races and quiz sprints. The same questions, a classroom that suddenly wakes up.

📊

Grades that stay private

Participation, behaviour and tests in one table, locked behind your own password so nobody peeks at the board.

Up and running in a minute

No setup, no installing. Make your teacher account, pick your class, and start.
